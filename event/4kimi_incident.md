# 4 Kimi Incident

The **4 Kimi Incident** was an assassination attempt against King [K’an Chitam](../people/kan_chitam.md) of Maya on July 16, 1910.

The perpetrator was a young Maya Pan-Americanist who idolized Savi and advocated closer ties with Mexica. During a public appearance, he attacked the king with a concealed obsidian knife, leaving K’an Chitam severely wounded.

The incident is named after the Tzolk'in date corresponding to July 16, 1910: **4 Kimi**.
