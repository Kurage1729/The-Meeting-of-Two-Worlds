# Other South American States

[Main page](../index.md) > [Countries](index.md) > Other South American states

This page collects countries for which only a short profile has currently been established. They can be separated into independent articles as the setting develops.

## Chile

| Category | Information |
|---|---|
| Capital | Santiago |
| Population | Approximately 19 million |
| Indigenous population | Approximately 50% |
| Languages | Spanish, Mapuche, and others |
| Territory | Real-world Chile excluding Tarapacá and Arica |

Southern Chile contains an extensive Mapuche autonomous territory.

## Argentina

| Category | Information |
|---|---|
| Capital | Buenos Aires |
| Population | Approximately 50 million |
| Indigenous population | Approximately 15% |
| Language | Spanish |
| Territory | Provisionally the same as in real history |

European migration that would have gone to the United States in real history was redirected toward Argentina, giving it a particularly large European-descended population. It contains a Mapuche autonomous region known as Puelmapu.

## Brazil

| Category | Information |
|---|---|
| Capital | Rio de Janeiro |
| Population | Approximately 140 million |
| Indigenous population | Approximately 8% |
| Language | Portuguese |

Brazil is considerably smaller than its real-world counterpart. Grão-Pará, Rio Grande do Sul, and Guaraní-inhabited regions broke away as Amazonia, Riograndense, and the eastern component of Paraguay.

## Paraguay

| Category | Information |
|---|---|
| Capital | Asunción |
| Population | Approximately 11 million |
| Indigenous population | Approximately 70% |
| Language | Guaraní |
| Territory | Real-world Paraguay, south-central Mato Grosso do Sul, and western Paraná |

Paraguay was formed through the union of Spanish Paraguay with the Guaraní-inhabited regions that separated from Portuguese Brazil.

## Guyana

| Category | Information |
|---|---|
| Capital | TBD |
| Population | Approximately 1 million; provisional |
| Indigenous population | Approximately 30% |
| Language | English |

Guyana extends much farther into areas claimed by real-world Venezuela and Brazil. Because Spanish and Portuguese colonization advanced less effectively, the states of the Guiana Shield are generally larger than in real history.

## Venezuela

Indigenous peoples make up approximately 40% of the population.

## Colombia

Indigenous peoples make up approximately 50% of the population.
