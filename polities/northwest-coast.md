## Formation

During the 1770s, the Chinook kingdoms and states extending from the Fraser River to the Tsimshian region formed a confederation. Haida kingdoms joined during the 1780s and 1790s.

Russian expansion led Tlingit clans to establish a military alliance in the 1790s. Following war with Russia around 1802–1804, the Tlingit kingdom formally joined the confederation.

## Oregon War

The confederation allied with Vinland during the [Oregon War](../history/oregon-war.md) of 1861. American victory forced it to cede the Chinook kingdoms and other territories south of the 48th parallel. The remaining confederation became a Vinlandic protectorate.

## Incorporation into Vinland

American–Vinlandic tension revived during the Alaska controversy of the 1880s. The confederation formally joined the Kingdom of Vinland while preserving its monarchy and extensive internal autonomy.
