# General Timeline

[Main page](index.md) > General timeline

This timeline summarizes major events across the TMTW setting. Links lead to regional, national, and event articles with more detail.

## Eleventh to thirteenth centuries

- **Early eleventh century** — Norse settlers establish a permanent colony in Newfoundland, bringing Eurasian pathogens, livestock, grain, and ironworking.
- **Mid-eleventh century** — Horses reach the Great Lakes region.
- **Eleventh–twelfth centuries** — Norse communities expand through Labrador and the St. Lawrence basin.
- **c. 1120** — [Kingdom of Vinland](countries/vinland.md) is founded at Mackinac.
- **Mid-twelfth century** — Vinlandic expeditions move down the Mississippi; wheat and ironworking reach the Mississippi basin.
- **Twelfth century** — Major epidemics spread through North America and Mesoamerica.
- **c. 1200** — A Vinlandic raid on the Huastec region marks the first known use of iron weapons in Mesoamerica.
- **Early thirteenth century** — A Vinlandic-derived state is founded near the Mississippi delta, strengthening trade with Mesoamerica.
- **Thirteenth century** — Epidemics reach South America.
- **Mid-thirteenth century** — Horses reach Mesoamerica.

## Fourteenth and fifteenth centuries

- **Early fourteenth century** — Tarascan smiths begin the first local iron production in Mesoamerica.
- **Mid-fourteenth century** — Mitlastchon Kingdom is founded in the Haudenosaunee region by a Vinlandic exile.
- **Late fourteenth century** — Other Mesoamerican states begin producing iron; horses reach the Andes.
- **Fourteenth–fifteenth centuries** — Pressure from Vinlandic-derived states and mounted bison hunting contribute to the formation of the Lakota.
- **Mid-fifteenth century** — The Haudenosaunee expel their king and establish a confederated republic.
- **Mid-fifteenth century** — A Towa-centered Pueblo confederation forms at Pecos.
- **Late fifteenth century** — Mexica expansion accelerates through the use of horses, wheat, and iron.
- **1486–1502** — Ahuitzotl conquers Tarascan territory and much of the northern Maya lowlands.
- **1502–1520** — Moctezuma II brings much of Aridoamerica into the Mexica tributary system.

## Sixteenth century

- **1519** — The first Spanish invasion of Mexica begins; Cortés fails to take Tenochtitlan but occupies Veracruz.
- **1520–1532** — Cuitláhuac reforms the Mexica tributary system and recovers Veracruz.
- **1532** — Spain invades the Inca Empire.
- **1540s** — A K’iche’-supported revolt liberates the Maya lowlands from Mexica rule; Spain takes Coatzacoalcos.
- **c. 1560** — A K’iche’-led united Maya state is established under a *Kaloomte’*.
- **1570** — Cusco falls, but New Inca successor states continue resistance.
- **Late sixteenth century** — The First Lakota Empire divides into northern and southern states.

## Seventeenth century

- **1600** — Tenochtitlan falls to Spain for the first time; the Mexica court withdraws to Metztitlan.
- **1620s** — The united Maya realm rebuilds Mayapán as its capital.
- **1621–1629** — A Dutch–Mexica alliance restores Tenochtitlan.
- **1630** — The last New Inca state is destroyed.
- **1648** — The Dutch end military support for Mexica.
- **1697** — Spain enters Tenochtitlan permanently, ending the Mexica Empire.

## Eighteenth century

- **c. 1700** — The [Inti religion](culture/inti-religion.md) develops into an organized movement in the Andes.
- **1702–1712** — Spain captures Dutch Gulf Coast fortresses during the War of the Spanish Succession.
- **1714** — The Dutch recognize Spanish possession of the former fortresses.
- **1710s–1720s** — Spain conquers the Guatemalan highlands.
- **Early eighteenth century** — The Second Lakota Empire reunifies much of the Plains.
- **1740s** — Spain conquers the northern Maya lowlands; the Maya court retreats to Nojpetén.
- **1770s–1800s** — Northwest Coast kingdoms form a confederation that eventually includes Haida and Tlingit members.
- **1780–1783** — Túpac Amaru III occupies Cusco but is defeated after a radical restoration program.
- **1776–1783** — The [United States](countries/united-states.md) wins independence.

## Nineteenth century

- **1818–1829** — Kaskaskia fights three wars against incorporation into the United States.
- **1821** — Mexica and the Peruvian highlands declare independence in separate movements.
- **1822** — Cacamatzin establishes the First Mexica Empire; Guatemala and Tiho accept temporary annexation by Mexica.
- **1824–1830** — Peru, Bolivia, and Ecuador secure independence.
- **1826** — The Sacred Quipu script is established; Chimalpilli begins the Second Mexica Empire.
- **1831** — The Maya restore an independent kingdom at Tiho; the Andean republics form an alliance.
- **1838** — The [Andean Federation](countries/andean-federation.md) and the independent [Kingdom of Vinland](countries/vinland.md) are established.
- **1839–1841** — Chile briefly occupies Lima but fails to destroy the Andean Federation.
- **1841** — The United States adopts the Western Territories Act.
- **1854** — Mexica sells the lower Rio Grande to the United States.
- **1855** — The [Mexica Republican Revolution](history/mexica-republican-revolution.md) abolishes the Second Empire and the nobility.
- **1861** — The [Oregon War](history/oregon-war.md) ends in American victory.
- **1866–1871** — The [American–Mexica War](history/american-mexica-war.md) ends with Mexica territorial losses.
- **1870s** — The Andean Federation is reorganized into ethnic and regional states.
- **1879–1884** — The Andean Federation loses Antofagasta but retains Tarapacá and Arica in the War of the Pacific.
- **1882** — Porforio Savi becomes president of Mexica.
- **1888** — K’an Chitam becomes *Kaloomte’* of the Maya.
- **1890s** — American Indigenous kingdoms are reorganized as Crowned States; Savi becomes president for life.
- **1895** — Savi proclaims the Third Mexica Empire.
- **1899–1902** — The Andean Federation retains Acre after defeating an Amazonian settler revolt.

## Twentieth century

- **1910** — [4 Kimi Incident](history/4-kimi-incident.md): K’an Chitam survives an assassination attempt.
- **1916** — Mexica enters the First World War on the side of the Central Powers and attacks the United States.
- **1917** — The Maya enter the war against Mexica.
- **1918** — Mexica is defeated; the Third Empire is abolished and the Second Republic begins.
- **1922** — The [6 B’en Revolution](history/6-ben-revolution.md) establishes constitutional government in the Maya kingdom.
- **1931** — An authoritarian military regime begins in the Maya kingdom.
- **1940** — Britain leases Pearl Harbor in Hawaii to the United States.
- **1941** — The [Huēyi Teōcalli Incident](history/hueyi-teocalli-incident.md) takes place one week after the Japanese attack on Pearl Harbor.
- **1944** — Mass protest ends the Maya dictatorship and reduces the monarchy to a symbolic institution.
- **1950** — A United Nations investigation discovers surviving human sacrifice in the Petén region.
- **1976** — Gale (Galagina) Saunooke becomes the first Indigenous president of the United States.
