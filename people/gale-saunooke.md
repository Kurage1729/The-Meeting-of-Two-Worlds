# Gale (Galagina) Saunooke

[Main page](../index.md) > [People](index.md) > Gale Saunooke

Gale, or Galagina, Saunooke was the first Indigenous president of the United States. He took office in 1976 and was of Cherokee background.

His election reflected the long rise of Cherokee citizens within the American federal system. Cherokee communities had experienced dispossession in the nineteenth-century Georgia gold rush but later achieved comparatively high levels of wealth and political influence.
