# Cacamatzin Chimal

[Main page](../index.md) > [People](index.md) > Cacamatzin Chimal

| Category | Information |
|---|---|
| Lifespan | 1779–1828 |
| Position | Tlatoani of Mexica, 1822–1826 |
| Known for | Victory in the independence civil war; founder of the First Empire |

Cacamatzin Chimal was born into a family of the middle Mexica nobility. A talented military commander, he defeated Iturbide during the [Mexica independence revolution](../history/mexica-independence.md) and took control of the Mexica noble army.

Many aristocrats wanted Chimalpilli of the old imperial family to take the throne. Cacamatzin's own faction instead supported its commander. In 1822, he proclaimed himself tlatoani and expelled rival factions.

His relatively modest ancestry made him vulnerable to accusations that he was an emperor without legitimate blood. The imperial faction deposed him in 1826 and replaced him with Chimalpilli. Cacamatzin was shot and killed in 1828.

The First Republic viewed him ambivalently because he had preserved the aristocratic system. During Savi's rule, the state recast him as a national hero of independence.
