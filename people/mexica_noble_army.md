# Mexica Noble Army

[Main page](../index.md) > [People](index.md) > Mexica Noble Army

The **Mexica Noble Army** was a military institution composed primarily of Indigenous nobles who cooperated with the Spanish colonial authorities in the Viceroyalty of México. Because Indigenous societies and populations remained considerably stronger in TMTW than in real history, the colonial government depended more heavily on Indigenous aristocratic military forces. Its composition and political role changed significantly over time.

Spanish rule was first firmly established in the Isthmus and Oaxaca, and during the 17th century the army was dominated by **Oaxacan nobles**, particularly Zapotec and Mixtec aristocratic families. Following the fall of Tenochtitlan, however, the Spanish reorganized the political structure of Central Mexico and established a hereditary Indigenous aristocracy headed by the **Tlatoani**, in order to maintain stability and facilitate indirect rule. This led to a long-standing rivalry between the older Oaxacan aristocracy and the emerging Central Mexican nobility.

During the 18th century, Central Mexican nobles who distinguished themselves in the conquest and administration of the Maya lowlands and the Pueblo regions gained increasing political and military influence. The Oaxacan aristocracy was consequently marginalized from central politics, although it retained considerable regional power.

The rivalry survived the independence of Mexica. Many Oaxacan nobles opposed the Tlatoani-centered aristocratic system and increasingly supported **republicanism**, viewing it as a means of restoring regional autonomy and limiting Central Mexican dominance. Their political opposition became one of the important forces behind the [Republican Revolution](../history/mexica-republican-revolution.md) of the mid-19th century.
