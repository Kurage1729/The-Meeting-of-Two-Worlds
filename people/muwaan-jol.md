Muwaan Jol was a Maya king who continued the centralization of the monarchy while accelerating the modernization of the Maya kingdom.

## Modernization

Muwaan Jol worked closely with bureaucrats drawn from the Pech aristocracy to advance modernization that had been delayed during the later years of Jasaw Chan Yopaat's reign. His government strengthened the administrative apparatus of the kingdom and promoted economic and institutional development.

## British–Maya War

In 1887, Muwaan Jol launched the British–Maya War with the aim of recovering Belize from Britain. Maya forces succeeded in occupying parts of the interior but were unable to advance into the heavily defended coastal region.

Muwaan Jol was killed during the war in 1888. His death brought his son K’an Chitam to the throne.

*Muwaan Jol's modernization and expansionist policies are loosely modeled on those of Justo Rufino Barrios in real-world Central America.*
