# Cecuiztli Tocal

[Main page](../index.md) > [People](index.md) > Cecuiztli Tocal

| Category | Information |
|---|---|
| Lifespan | 1805–1887 |
| Position | First president of the First Mexica Republic |
| Known for | Leading republican thinker; “Father of the Republic” |

Cecuiztli Tocal was born into an impoverished family of lower Tlaxcalan nobility. He welcomed Mexica independence during the 1820s but became disillusioned when the new state preserved the colonial aristocratic order.

He joined the republican movement during the 1840s and became one of its principal leaders. His willingness to promote supporters without regard to ethnicity attracted many Zapotec and Mixtec activists.

After the [Mexica Republican Revolution](../history/mexica-republican-revolution.md) succeeded in 1855, Cecuiztli became the first president of the First Republic. He returned to the presidency several times but could not fully contain ethnic and political conflict after the American–Mexica War.

In retirement, he criticized the growth of Pan-Americanism and was especially hostile to Porforio Savi.
