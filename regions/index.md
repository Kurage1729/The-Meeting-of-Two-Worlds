# Regions

[Main page](../index.md) > Regions

The history of TMTW can be approached through three broad regions. Each regional article provides an overview and links to the countries, peoples, and events discussed in greater detail elsewhere.

- [North America](north-america.md) — Vinland, the United States, the Mississippi states, the Plains, the Pueblo region, and the Northwest Coast.
- [Mesoamerica](mesoamerica.md) — Mexica, Maya, the Spanish conquest, and the survival of Mesoamerican institutions.
- [South America](south-america.md) — the Andes, Amazonia, the Guaraní region, and the postcolonial fragmentation of Portuguese and Spanish America.
